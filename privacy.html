Privacy Policy

SwiftMind collects nothing. This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. SwiftMind does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account to create and no sign-in.

Network activity

The app makes no network requests. The entire learning catalogue — concepts, diagrams, code examples, quizzes, trivia and interview questions — is bundled inside the app and read from the device. The app functions identically in airplane mode.

Analytics and third parties

There are no analytics, no crash-reporting services, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind embedded in the app.

Your data

Your learning progress — concepts read, quiz results, streaks, and the spaced-review schedule — is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded. It is included in your device backup if you have backups enabled, and it is permanently deleted when you delete the app.

The AI tutor

The in-app tutor rephrases and re-presents the same bundled content. It does not send anything you read or type to a server, and it is not backed by a cloud model.

Children

SwiftMind is a technical learning tool suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
