Support

SwiftMind is an offline-first iPhone and iPad app for learning iOS concepts. If something is broken, wrong, or missing, tell me — content corrections are especially welcome.

Get in touch

  • Email: mkamaalg4@gmail.com
  • Bugs and feature requests: GitHub issues

For a bug report, the app version and your iOS version help a lot. For a content correction, the concept title is enough.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 26.5 or later
  • No internet connection required, ever

Frequently asked

Does it work offline?
Entirely. The whole catalogue ships inside the app and the app makes no network requests at all.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-in and no account to create.
Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. It is included in device backups and is deleted when you delete the app.
Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Not yet. Progress is per-device today. The app itself runs natively on both.
How do I reset my progress?
Profile tab → “Reset all progress”. Deleting the app also clears everything.
Is new content added over time?
Yes. Concepts, quizzes and interview questions arrive with app updates, so an update is all you need to get them.
Can I suggest a concept?
Please do — email or open an issue with the topic and why it tripped you up. The confusing ones make the best entries.
